How do I buy 0ms?

What is a lifetime key?

One payment gets you a key that never expires and is bound to your specific hardware (HWID), so it can't be shared.

Does 0ms reduce my real ping?

It applies real local TCP/DNS and system tuning to reduce packet-processing delay and input latency. It cannot change your physical distance to a game server.

Is it safe / reversible?

Yes. Every change is a standard Windows setting that can be toggled off or rolled back with a restore point.

Can I run it in the background?

Absolutely — it minimizes to your system tray and keeps working silently while you play.
